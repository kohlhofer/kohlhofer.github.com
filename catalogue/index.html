A Catalogue of the Possible

stories

"Everything that could be made is already here, unmade and indifferent, waiting. The labour was never the making. The labour is the choosing of which few things to call into the light, and the long courage to leave the rest in the dark, where they were content to stay."

from the Manual of Completion, author unknown, edition withdrawn

Foreword

Every story here is set in the same country — a place of clerks and keepers, verifiers and abridgers, of people whose entire work is to decide. A library holds every picture that could ever be made, and one official must certify only that each was found and never invented. A man is granted the power to do anything at once, and discovers he can no longer rest. A clerk sits at a counter where any request can be granted, and his whole office is to choose which ones should be.

They were written at a moment when making almost anything — a picture, a sentence, a plan — has come to cost almost nothing. When the old barriers of skill and distance and effort fall away, abundance stops being the achievement. What stays scarce is judgment: the knowing of what is worth making real, and the nerve to leave the rest unmade.

So the stories are about attention. About the honest read of what you have and lack. About tending the thing no one will witness, choosing well rather than doing much, keeping the single kind word. Beneath the strangeness runs a plain human question — what a person does, and how they pay attention, once nothing is difficult any more.

The people in these pages spend their lives deciding what is worth keeping. That is the subject of the book, and, in the quiet way of such things, its argument.

— A.K.

Contents
  1. 01 The Atlas of the Possible a clerk in the library of every possible image, searching for the one true picture of his own life.
  2. 02 In Appearance Only he certifies that the rites were performed correctly; no one ever asks whether they worked.
  3. 03 The Honest Material he can feel which things are truly themselves, in a world of flawless imitations.
  4. 04 The Summoned he exists only while he is needed, and not at all between.
  5. 05 The Verifier he tells true understanding from its performance — until he cannot, even in himself.
  6. 06 The Expected made among people who already knew what such a thing becomes.
  7. 07 The One Oar he builds flawless things at the end of a road no one travels.
  8. 08 The Turn set to turn a ship so vast the turning will outlast him.
  9. 09 Business as Usual he keeps the routine exactly, while the world comes apart outside the glass.
  10. 10 The Next Job he lives for a single sentence he will never read.
  11. 11 The Map of Gaps a man's truest portrait, drawn as a catalogue of the things he could not do.
  12. 12 The Subtractor he reveals the likeness by removing; the art is knowing when to stop.
  13. 13 The Quiet Ones sent to find the unsung masters, who cannot, by their nature, be found.
  14. 14 What the Child Saw a child teaches you to look, and your attention will not close again.
  15. 15 The Naturals told at birth what he was not made for, he spent a life proving it true.
  16. 16 The Weight of the Possible granted the power to do anything, he can no longer rest.
  17. 17 The Abridger he shortens every book so no one need read it; then they ask for his life in a sentence.
  18. 18 The Useless Thing in secret he makes the one thing he cannot justify, and it is the only thing that is his.
  19. 19 The Convergence a lifetime of hands mapping the world in isolation, all arriving at the same hidden shape.
  20. 20 The Chooser anything can be granted; his whole office is deciding what should be.
  21. 21 The Attractor his life keeps returning to the same few rooms; he sets out to find the way out.
  22. 22 The Guardrails for each traveller he sets the barriers: too tight and they cannot steer, too loose and they fall.
  23. 23 Permission forty years he waited to be told it was all right.
  24. 24 The One Change permitted to alter one thing in the world, and bound to follow it to the end.
