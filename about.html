How I work

Direction before speed: a team moving fast in the wrong direction just reaches the wrong place sooner. I set the heading first, clear what is slowing people down, and push on pace last.

Outcomes over output. A team can ship constantly and change nothing, and AI makes that easier than ever — it can fill every proxy metric we have at no more real value. So the question stays the same: did the customer's problem go away, did the business move.

Hire for judgment. When execution gets cheap, judgment gets scarce; the valuable person is the one who can tell which of a hundred generated options is right. That applies to me too — my job is to make the team complete, which only works if I can name where I am weak.

There is a longer version of all this in how I lead.

Still building

I never stopped making things myself. At Field Bureau + Werkstatt I design and ship the tools directly — Signal Recorder SR-7, Markdown Library ML-42, Task Register TR-2 — with a small pod working alongside AI.

It keeps my hands on the new economics firsthand. A handful of people can now hold an entire stack and move at a pace that used to take a department. Leading well through that shift is hard to fake from the sidelines.
